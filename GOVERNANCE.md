# Governance

## Roles
- **Contributors**: anyone who opens an issue, a PR or a discussion.
- **Maintainers**: review and merge PRs in one or more areas (firmware, hardware, base, docs). New maintainers are invited after sustained, quality contributions.
- **Project lead**: sets direction and breaks ties. Currently the founder.

## Decisions
- Day-to-day decisions happen in PRs and issues, by consensus.
- Significant decisions (architecture, protocol, licensing, alert logic) go through an **RFC**: a Discussion in the *RFCs* category, left open for at least 7 days.
- Safety-critical changes need 2 maintainer approvals (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

As the community grows, this document will change to add area leads, a steering group and possibly a fiscal host.
